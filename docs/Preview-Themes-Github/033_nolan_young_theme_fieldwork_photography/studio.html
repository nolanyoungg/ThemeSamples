A studio with room for life

There is a story in
the things you already know.

The dog who waits by the door. The room you spent months making your own. Your family together on a perfectly ordinary afternoon. We think those things deserve a considered photograph.

Fieldwork is a fictional studio built around five complementary services. The idea is simple: a friendly, useful experience from the first conversation to the photographs you come back to.

Three things we come back to

Be present.
Be thoughtful.

01 / BEFORE THE CAMERA

A plan, not a performance.

We talk about the people, place, and purpose of the photographs. A little preparation gives everyone room to relax.

02 / IN THE MOMENT

Direction when it helps.

Clear suggestions, a patient pace, and enough flexibility to notice when the best picture is happening somewhere else.

03 / AFTER THE SESSION

A considered edit.

A coherent collection of useful photographs, with attention to the small details that make them feel like they belong together.

A little clarity

Is this a real photography business?

Fieldwork is an original website design preview. Services, package prices, and fulfilment options are illustrative. The enquiry and checkout forms are local demonstrations; they do not contact a photographer or place an order.

Who made the portfolio photographs?

The service and portfolio images are licensed Pexels photographs, credited to their original photographers. They illustrate the visual direction and are not represented as commissions photographed by Fieldwork.

Can the print-shop photographs be reproduced?

The three archive images are identified by The Met as public domain and provided through its CC0 Open Access programme. Their original artists, object records, and license references are listed in the photo source record.

What information does the preview save?

Only the selected print IDs, sizes, frames, finishes, and quantities are stored in this browser’s local bag. Enquiry details are never sent or saved. Demo checkout asks for no real address or payment details.

Can I plan prints from a session?

Mention the sizes or wall space you have in mind in the session enquiry. This preview’s purchasable sample collection uses public-domain archive photographs; the licensed stock portfolio images are not offered for sale as prints.
