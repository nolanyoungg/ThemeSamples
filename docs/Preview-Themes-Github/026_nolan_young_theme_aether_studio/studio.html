01 / Find the feeling

Art direction,
in your own words.

The most useful prompt isn't the longest one. It's the one that knows what it wants to feel like.

Begin with your subject. Give it a material, a setting, and a point of view. “A silver object” becomes “a liquid silver ribbon, suspended in a quiet lilac room.” That specificity makes a direction worth pursuing.

A SMALL PROMPT RECIPE

Subject + atmosphere
+ a little surprise.

Subject: A sculptural chrome loop.
Atmosphere: Soft violet light, a quiet room.
Surprise: A glass sphere suspended within.

Room for the
whole process.

A few thoughtful tools, designed to stay out of your way.

01 / COLLECT

Keep a thread.

Gather visual references and draft directions into collections, so the reason behind an idea stays as clear as the image itself.

02 / REFINE

Change one thing.

Explore a warmer light, a different material, or a wider view. Small changes help you understand which decisions are doing the work.

03 / SHARE

Invite a new eye.

Bring collaborators into a considered selection, with the context they need to give clear, useful creative feedback.
