A little more clarity.

Good tools should feel straightforward, right from the start.

Can I explore before choosing a plan?+

Yes. The interactive demonstrations are open to everyone. Try the workflow, explore the product, and choose a starting point when you are ready.

What happens when my team grows?+

The sample plans illustrate a path from individual work to a shared workspace. A production product could support adding members without moving your existing projects.

What does annual billing mean?+

The yearly option displays the monthly equivalent of a twelve-month commitment at a 20% discount. These are illustrative prices; this site does not collect payment.

Does this preview send my information anywhere?+

No. All demonstrations run in your browser. Forms show local confirmation only, and there is no account service or payment processor connected.
