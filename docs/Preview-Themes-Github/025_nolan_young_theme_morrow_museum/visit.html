How long should I allow?

Around 90 minutes gives you time for the featured installation and a walk through the collection. A shorter visit can focus on one gallery.

Can I bring children?

The sample programme includes a family studio and simple looking activities. Children remain with their accompanying adults.

Will this reserve a ticket?

No. The planner calculates an illustrative total and displays a local confirmation. It does not make a booking or take payment.
