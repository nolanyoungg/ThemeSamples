SEASON 01 / MAIN HALL

Blue Assembly

A suspended field of cobalt fabric changes as visitors move beneath it. Light passes through overlapping layers, turning a familiar gallery into a landscape of shifting colour.

COLLECTION / GALLERIES 2–4

Form & Presence

Carved stone and cast bronze share a sequence of sunlit rooms. The display invites visitors to consider weight, surface, and the way sculpture changes our experience of space.

Allow around 45 minutes for a quiet walk through the collection. Seating is included along the route.
