Featured exhibition / Main hall

Blue
Assembly

An imagined installation exploring how colour, light, and movement transform a shared space.

Experience

Plan your visit ↗

Not just something
to look at.

Blue Assembly is conceived as a work you encounter with your whole body. Broad ribbons of translucent fabric descend, curve, and rise through the hall, creating different views as you move.

The sculpture has no single preferred viewpoint. From one position, the layers appear dense and saturated; from another, daylight opens them into pale, overlapping planes.

Make time to notice

Look upward, then look through the work toward another part of the room. Notice how a change in position changes the apparent depth and colour.

Visiting with children

Try finding three different shades of blue. Talk about which parts seem heavy or light, still or moving. The installation is for looking and walking around, rather than touching.
