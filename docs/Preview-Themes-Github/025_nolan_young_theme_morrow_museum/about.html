About Morrow

A place for
open minds.

Morrow is an imagined museum of contemporary art, built around the belief that looking together can change what we see.

Art is a beginning.

We bring works, ideas, and people into conversation. An exhibition can be a place to encounter something unfamiliar, notice an ordinary detail, or find a question worth keeping.

Our fictional programme connects large-scale installations with quieter experiences of the collection. Alongside the galleries, talks and family activities offer different ways into the work.
