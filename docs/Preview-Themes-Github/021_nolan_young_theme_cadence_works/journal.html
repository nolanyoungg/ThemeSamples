01 / Workshop

The case for keeping it

A familiar bike carries more than its components. It carries routes, habits, and the little adjustments that make it yours.

Our design philosophy favours understanding the whole machine before deciding what to change. These are editorial concepts, not repair instructions.

02 / On the road

The everyday essentials

The most useful additions are often the least dramatic. A place for a bag, room for changing weather, and contact points that suit your ride.

A city bike and a weekend bike may share more priorities than you think. Start with the journeys you make most often.

Continue the conversation ↗

03 / Design

A build begins with a route

Before choosing parts, imagine the road. The surface, the hills, the distance, and what you want to carry make the brief.

The resulting build should feel coherent. Every part earns its place by helping the whole bike do its job.
