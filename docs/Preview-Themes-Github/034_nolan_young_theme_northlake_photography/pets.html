GOOD TO KNOW

A few
small answers.

Do they need to be trained?

No. Movement and curiosity are welcome. We build the session around what your companion is comfortable doing.

Can my pet stay on a lead?

Absolutely. We plan compositions with the lead in mind. Safety and comfort come first.

Do you photograph cats?

Yes. A quiet session in their own home often gives cats the space they need.
