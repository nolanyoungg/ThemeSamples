THE SMALL DETAILS

Come prepared.
Leave the pressure.

01 / WHAT TO WEAR

Start with something you love.

Favorite clothes tend to photograph well because you feel like yourself in them. Think soft layers, comfortable shoes, and colors that sit easily beside one another.

Muted cream, denim, olive, and warm brown work with an autumn setting without asking everyone to match.

02 / BRINGING THE FAMILY

Make the day a little easier.

Keep the hour before your session simple. A meal, a nap, and some extra travel time can do more than any list of poses.

Bring a snack and an extra layer. If someone needs a break, we take one. The photograph can wait.

03 / BRINGING YOUR PET

Familiar things help.

A usual lead, a favorite toy, a small supply of treats, and water. Choose a setting where your companion can feel comfortable.

Tell us about sensitivities or mobility needs in advance so the plan works for them, too.

04 / PROPERTY PREPARATION

Let the space breathe.

Clear the everyday clutter, open curtains, and give rooms a quick walk-through from the doorway. Small distractions are easier to remove before the camera comes out.

Have a short list of the details that make the property special. We will build them into the story.
