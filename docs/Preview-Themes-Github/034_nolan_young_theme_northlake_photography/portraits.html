GOOD TO KNOW

A few
small answers.

I feel awkward in photographs. Is that okay?

Very. We use simple movement and small prompts, with time to settle in rather than a list of rigid poses.

Can these be used professionally?

Personal branding and professional portraits are welcome. We discuss the intended usage and image formats before the shoot.

Can I bring someone?

Yes. A familiar person can help you feel at ease; let us know who will be joining.
