After Hours brings the warmth of a living room to the ritual of a listening bar. A carefully placed sound system, a thoughtful drinks list, and records given the space to play.

The bar takes the same approach as the music: a focused selection, carefully prepared. Familiar combinations sit alongside something a little less expected, with equal attention given to alcohol-free choices.
