The practice

A shared attention
to place.

STONEFORM is a fictional independent architecture practice with a focus on public life, thoughtful materials, and enduring usefulness.

Architecture is
a collective act.

A building emerges from many kinds of knowledge: the people who will use it, the constraints of a site, the skills of makers, and the responsibilities of a client.

Our approach makes room for those perspectives early. We use drawings, models, and material studies to turn a broad ambition into a coherent set of decisions.

A good building makes its purpose clear without exhausting its possibilities.

01 / Context

Begin by looking.

Understand the setting, patterns of use, and qualities that should be retained.

02 / Collaboration

Make ideas discussable.

Use clear design material to bring clients, users, and specialist knowledge into the process.

03 / Craft

Carry intent into detail.

Resolve material junctions and everyday touchpoints as part of the architectural whole.

A place begins
with a conversation.
