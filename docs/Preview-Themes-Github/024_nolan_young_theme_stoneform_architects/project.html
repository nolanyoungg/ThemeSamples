Project 01 / Cultural architecture

The Tidal Centre

A public building at the meeting point of land, water, and cultural life.

Type
Civic & cultural
Setting
Coastal landscape
Materials
Concrete / oak / stone
Status
Fictional concept study

A public threshold

Between the landscape
and the life within.

The centre is imagined as a series of simple volumes arranged along a sheltered route. Rather than creating a single monumental entrance, the design offers a gradual transition from open landscape to protected interior.

A reflecting pool extends the horizon into the courtyard. Deep window reveals temper the light, while timber surfaces mark the places where people gather and pause.

A clear material language

The palette is deliberately limited. Pale concrete gives the building a sense of permanence; oak introduces warmth at the human scale. Stone paving carries the public route through the site.

Designed around
everyday public life.

Flexible gathering rooms sit beside quieter spaces for reading, discussion, and small exhibitions. The public route remains legible from arrival, with the landscape serving as a consistent point of orientation.

This is an architectural design study. The images and project description illustrate a fictional proposal rather than a completed commission.
