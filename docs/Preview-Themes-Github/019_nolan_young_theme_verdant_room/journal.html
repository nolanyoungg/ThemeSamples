01 / Plant wisdom

Start with the light

Before choosing a plant, watch the room for a day. A bright window in the morning can feel very different by late afternoon.

Our first sketch always includes light and everyday routines. Good planting is a relationship between a living thing and the place it will live.

02 / Objects

A pot with a purpose

A vessel has a practical job before it has a decorative one. Drainage, stability, and room for roots all shape the choice.

Warm clay, simple glazed ceramics, and quiet colours let the foliage lead. Variation in height gives a group a more natural rhythm.

03 / Spaces

Less, but leafier

One well-placed plant can change the balance of a room. Start with a strong silhouette and leave some space around it.

Try looking at the room from the doorway. Where does your eye rest? That is often a better guide than filling every empty corner.
