Experiences / Three ways to look closer

Find your
kind of night.

A first look through the telescope. A walk through an impossible world. A quieter evening with a few good people. Begin where your curiosity takes you.

3 experiences

La Silla telescope · Zdeněk Bardon/ESO · CC BY 4.0

01 / The open dome

A first look.
A lasting feeling.

The evening begins with a conversation. Your guide introduces the space, the instrument, and a few ways to look at what is above us. Then the dome opens, the room becomes quieter, and the sky takes its turn.

There is time to ask the small questions as well as the large ones. No prior astronomy knowledge is needed; the experience is built around curiosity, rather than a test of what you already know.

Duration90 minutesSample admission$38 adult / $18 childBest forFirst-time explorers

02 / Worlds in motion

A different
sense of scale.

Walk beneath a ringed world. Follow a line of light. See how the same object can become something entirely different when your point of view changes.

This artist-imagined exhibition brings the language of the night sky into a physical space. It invites slow wandering, shared discoveries, and the freedom to make your own connections.

Suggested visit60 minutesSample admission$24 adult / $12 childSettingIndoor gallery

03 / A quieter hour

Make the evening
your own.

A private introduction to Nocturne for a small group. Follow your questions, spend longer with an exhibit, or make the telescope the centre of your night.

A dedicated guide shapes the pace around your group. The concept is intentionally small: fewer people, more time, and room for a conversation that can go somewhere unexpected.

Duration120 minutesSample admission$95 adult / $45 childGroup sizeUp to 8 adults

Come as
you are.

A sense of curiosity is the only starting point you need.

Is an observing session right for a first visit?

Yes. The imagined open-dome programme begins with an introduction and makes time for questions. You do not need equipment or specialist knowledge.

What if the sky is cloudy?

The sample programme pairs observing with gallery time. In the concept, a cloudy evening becomes a guided exploration of the exhibition and the instrument rather than a promise of a particular view.

Can I choose a step-free route?

The visit planner includes a step-free preference. The sample itinerary then uses the gallery, terrace, and a telescope-viewing screen in place of the upper instrument platform.
