01 / A wider view

In a world that never stops,
spend a little time
looking up.

Nocturne is a place for the questions that arrive when everything else becomes quiet. A mountain observatory, an immersive gallery, and an open invitation to see the familiar differently.

You don't need to know the names of the stars. Bring your curiosity. Our guides, spaces, and evenings are designed to make that first look feel like the beginning of something.
